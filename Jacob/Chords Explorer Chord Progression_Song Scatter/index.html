Data is read straight from chordonomicon_spotifydump_join.csv.gz in the Chords-Explorer folder (decompressed in your browser). The staff shows the 16 chords found in the most songs. Seventh, slash and other extended chords count as their plain major or minor chord, and repeated chords in a row count once, so G–D means G followed directly by D. Each dot is one song, placed by release date, sized by Spotify popularity and colored by main genre; vertical position is random. Songs with no release date or from before 1950 are left out.
