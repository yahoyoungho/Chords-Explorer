Chordonomicon + Spotify

Which songs use this chord progression?

Pick a note and a ♭, ♮ or ♯ from the dropdowns (or click a note on the staff), choose major or minor, and the chord lifts above the staff to join your progression (up to 4, in order). The songs that play those chords back to back, in that order, pop up on the timeline below. Under each note the staff shows the chord that most often comes next.

Data is read straight from Erik/chordonomicon_transformed.csv.gz in the Chords-Explorer folder (decompressed in your browser). Seventh, slash, suspended and other extended chords count as their plain major or minor chord; sharps and flats are the same chord (F♯ = G♭); and repeated chords in a row count once, so G–D means G followed directly by D. Each dot is one song, placed by release date, sized by Spotify popularity and colored by main genre; vertical position is random. Songs with no release date or from before 1950 are left out.
